The Quantized Dimensional Ledger

The Quantized Dimensional Ledger (QDL) interprets dimensional structure as a lattice of discrete dimensional charges. Physically admissible operators correspond to combinations whose dimensional charges cancel, forming the neutral closure sector of the lattice.

Core Idea

Core Principle

Dimensional quantities form a charge lattice; admissible operators lie in its neutral sector.

The QDL framework maps dimensional quantities into an integer lattice of dimensional charges. Physically admissible operators are those combinations whose dimensional charges sum to zero.

QDL Master Relation
(Q, ·) → Φ → (ℤⁿ, +)
Admissible operators: A = ker Φ

Instant Idea

Conceptual Summary
The Quantized Dimensional Ledger maps dimensional quantities into an integer lattice of dimensional charges. Physical operators correspond to combinations whose charges cancel.
Technical Overview
Dimensional quantities expressed in a unit basis map to integer exponent vectors through a dimension homomorphism Φ. Multiplication corresponds to addition of charge vectors. Physically admissible operators correspond to the neutral closure sector given by ker Φ.
