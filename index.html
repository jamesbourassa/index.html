The Quantized Dimensional Ledger

Standard dimensional analysis tests consistency, but it does not determine whether a representation is structurally admissible. The Quantized Dimensional Ledger (QDL) explores dimensional closure as a structural admissibility constraint on physical operators and measurement relations.

Dimensional charge lattice Neutral closure sector Admissible operators Structural closure

Core Idea

Core Principle

Dimensional quantities form a charge lattice; admissible operators lie in its neutral sector.

The Quantized Dimensional Ledger maps dimensional quantities into an integer lattice of dimensional charges. Physically admissible operators correspond to combinations whose dimensional charges cancel.

QDL Master Relation
(Q, ·) → Φ → (ℤⁿ, +)
Admissible operators: A = ker Φ

Dimensional quantities map into integer charge vectors under the homomorphism Φ. The admissible operator sector corresponds to the neutral closure sector ker Φ.
