The Mathematical Starting Point

The central QDL mathematical definitions are independently inspectable and do not require accepting a proposed microscopic physical ontology.

Five-Coordinate Integer Ledger

A QDL ledger state is defined as:

w = (L₁, L₂, L₃; F₁, F₂) ∈ ℤ⁵

The primitive rank-four closure condition is:

C(w) = 2(L₁ + L₂ + L₃) − 3(F₁ + F₂) = 0

Every closed integer vector has the form:

w = (k+p, k+q, k−p−q; k+t, k−t)
k, p, q, t ∈ ℤ

Its projection satisfies:

(ΣL, ΣF) = (3k, 2k)

The resulting dimensional class is associated with the QDL primitive L³F².

Structural Admissibility Before Physical Dynamics

QDL separates the question of whether a proposed mathematical relation is admissible under a declared rule from the question of whether the relation describes an actual physical process.

This distinction supports reproducible calculations, rule-based software tests, and mathematical auditing.

Important calculator distinction: the public calculator's default exact-membership rule checks the stricter subgroup w = k(1,1,1,1,1).

This is not equivalent to testing the entire rank-four closure lattice C(w) = 0. Both are defined mathematical tests with different admissible sets.

A successful closure check establishes consistency with the selected rules. It does not by itself demonstrate unique physical necessity.

Three Complementary Research Directions

The QDL program is organized so that the value of established mathematics and metrology does not depend on completing the Unified Field Theory.

Mathematical Foundations

Integer closure, finite roots, reversible contact algebra, graph geometry, lattice representations, and mathematical consistency conditions.

Evaluation standard: proofs, declared axioms, mathematical originality, and reproducibility.

Metrology and Model Validation

Typed measurement relationships, structural admissibility, computational audits, scientific-software verification, and model-consistency checking.

Evaluation standard: independent test cases, conventional comparators, defect detection, false-positive rates, and incremental value.

Foundational Physics

Investigation of QDCs as a hypothetical physical substrate, with possible consequences for particles, collective interactions, gravity, and spacetime.

Evaluation standard: physically identified degrees of freedom, justified interactions, and independently testable predictions.

Measurement Integrity and Scientific-Model Validation

A practical evaluation opportunity that does not require prior acceptance of the QDC spacetime hypothesis.

The QDL Measurement Integrity Engine is an executable research prototype for evaluating whether declared scientific and measurement relationships satisfy specified structural rules.

Development has included source-code audits, corrected implementation candidates, regression testing, and an internal public-measurement pilot using published determinations of Newton's gravitational constant.

The pilot demonstrated executable checking of declared dimensions, but did not establish superiority over an appropriate conventional dimensional checker.

Further development and independent evaluation would need to address physically justified axis assignments, quantity kinds, uncertainty, reference frames, provenance, and applicable covariance relationships.

Proposed independent evaluation: compare a frozen QDL software implementation against established dimensional-analysis and semantic-validation methods using independently authored and adjudicated cases.

Report detection rates, false positives, missed errors, abstentions, runtime, and reproducible audit traces.

No independent blinded comparative evaluation has yet been completed.

The QDC Spacetime-Substrate Hypothesis

A long-term foundational research direction whose physical interpretation remains unverified.

What the Hypothesis Proposes

Quantized Dimensional Cells may constitute an underlying relational substrate from which effective geometry, collective fields, and particle-like behavior could emerge.

QDL also investigates whether related structural relationships recur across quantum, atomic, biological, gravitational, and cosmological scales.

Shared dimensions or cyclic behavior alone do not demonstrate that these processes arise from the same microscopic physical constituents.

What Physical Evidence Is Still Required?

A physical completion would require:

1. An independently identified physical QDC-like constituent or operational carrier.

2. A justified interaction law producing measurable collective dynamics.

3. A distinctive quantitative prediction distinguishable from established theory.

Without those connections, the mathematical models do not establish a physically completed Unified Field Theory.
