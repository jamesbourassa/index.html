One Mathematical Audit Framework, Many Potential Physical Systems

Many physical systems exhibit recurrence, coupling, conservation, feedback, or closed-path behavior. QDL asks whether some can be represented by explicitly assigned integer ledgers and whether the same closure criteria reveal useful invariants, incompatible assumptions, or new independently testable relationships.

The key conceptual distinction: QDL as a mathematical and measurement-audit method need not imply that every system is made of QDCs. Its application to a larger or smaller physical system must be justified by that system's own variables, dimensions, symmetries, and dynamics—not by an assumed universal microscopic substrate.

1. Define a physical systemSpecify measured variables, model boundaries, quantity kinds, dynamics, and genuine closure relations.
2. Apply an explicit QDL mapDeclare the ledger assignment and independently evaluate the closure or admissibility test.
3. Compare and predictBenchmark against established methods and seek a prospective, independently measurable consequence.

Candidate test classes: oscillators and resonators, coupled electrical networks, transport and circulation models, orbital or gravitational relationships, wave systems, and multiscale feedback models. These are proposed test domains, not demonstrated QDL solutions.

Critical falsification rule: a closed loop, an L³F² dimensional resemblance, or a fit to known data does not automatically establish QDL-specific physics. The proposed mapping must add something independently verifiable beyond ordinary dimensional analysis and standard conservation or dynamical equations.

The Mathematical Starting Point: L³F²

The central QDL definitions can be evaluated as formal mathematics without presupposing a physical QDC ontology.

Five-Coordinate Integer Ledger

A ledger state uses three length-like and two frequency-like integer coordinates:

w = (L₁, L₂, L₃; F₁, F₂) ∈ ℤ⁵

Its primitive rank-four closure lattice is defined by:

C(w) = 2(L₁ + L₂ + L₃) − 3(F₁ + F₂) = 0

Every integer solution has a parametrization:

w = (k+p, k+q, k−p−q; k+t, k−t)
k, p, q, t ∈ ℤ

The summed-coordinate projection is:

(ΣL, ΣF) = (3k, 2k)

This yields the defining QDL primitive dimensional class L³F², under its declared ledger interpretation.

Admissibility Is Not the Same as Physical Necessity

QDL distinguishes a formal statement—this vector satisfies a declared closure rule—from a physical statement—nature must obey this rule in this system.

That separation makes mathematical consistency, source-data integrity, model semantics, and experimental evidence independently auditable.

Calculator distinction: the public calculator's default exact-membership test uses the stricter subgroup:

w = k(1,1,1,1,1)

This is not equivalent to testing the entire rank-four lattice C(w) = 0. A valid subgroup result does not automatically classify all closed vectors.

Neither closure identity alone nor a match of dimensions supplies a physical equation of motion, interaction amplitude, or unique observable prediction.

Measurement Integrity: A Practical Independent Test

Operational QDL checks can be assessed without first accepting a QDC hypothesis.

The QDL Measurement Integrity Engine is a research prototype for testing whether declared measurement and model relationships comply with explicit structural constraints. Work to date includes code audits, regression tests, validation traces, and an internal pilot using published measurements of Newton's gravitational constant.

The pilot established that declared dimensions could be checked in a reproducible workflow. It did not establish superiority over conventional dimensional-checking software.

Further evaluations must independently specify quantity kinds, physically justified ledger assignments, uncertainties, provenance, reference frames, and applicable covariance relations.

Proposed independent benchmark: freeze a QDL implementation, use independently authored and adjudicated test cases, compare it with appropriate conventional dimensional and semantic validators, and report detection rates, false positives, missed errors, abstentions, runtime, and reproducible audit traces.

No independently blinded comparative benchmark has yet established an incremental advantage.

What the Physical Hypothesis Proposes

Quantized Dimensional Cells could form a relational substrate from which effective geometry, collective fields, and particle-like behavior emerge.

The proposal is a conditional research hypothesis. Similar closed-loop features at large and small scales would not, on their own, prove shared microscopic constituents.

What Would Establish Physical Progress?

1. Independently identify a physically meaningful carrier or degree of freedom.

2. Justify an interaction law that derives real collective spectra and quantitative amplitudes.

3. Produce a distinctive, prospective prediction and obtain independent experimental verification.

The complete Standard Model, gravitation, and observed constants have not been uniquely derived from the starting dimensional identity.

Independent Scientific Evaluation Is Welcome

The QDL Physics Institute invites qualified mathematicians, physicists, measurement scientists, experimental groups, and scientific-software researchers to examine its openly accessible work.

Three Evaluation Priorities

Mathematics: reproduce closure, lattice, root, contact, and finite-network results; assess novelty and declared assumptions.

Metrology and cross-scale methods: test frozen software and mappings against independent cases, established comparators, controlled predictions, and known failure modes.

Physical foundations: scrutinize the missing microscopic carrier, interaction law, amplitudes, and experimental predictions rather than treating formal compatibility as physical confirmation.

The desired result is an impartial written scientific assessment. No federal endorsement, experimentally confirmed QDC ontology, or independently established software superiority is claimed.
