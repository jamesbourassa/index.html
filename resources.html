Independent Technical Review: Start Here

The Institute welcomes independent written assessment of three separable research subjects. A reviewer need not accept the QDC spacetime hypothesis in order to evaluate the mathematics or measurement software.

A. Mathematical Audit

Verify the closure parametrization, native-root count, reversible-contact identities and declared finite-network certificates. Assess mathematical novelty against existing literature.

B. Measurement-Integrity Pilot

Compare a frozen QDL software version with conventional dimensional, semantic-type and workflow validators on independently adjudicated cases.

C. Physics Evidence Review

Separate conditional QDC constructions from physically motivated dynamics, evaluate proposed discriminants and identify the missing evidence for a physical carrier.

Review objective: a documented technical finding, including limitations and negative results—not an endorsement of unverified physical unification.
