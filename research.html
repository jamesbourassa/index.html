QDL Research: Exact Mathematics, Cross-Scale Tests and Open Physical Questions

The Quantized Dimensional Ledger (QDL) program develops exact dimensional-closure mathematics, reproducible computational constructions, and structural-admissibility methods for measurement and physical models.

Current public synthesis: Quantized Dimensional Ledger Unified Field Theory, Version V2.2, by James D. Bourassa (2026), Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23838420.

New research direction: test whether QDL methods can identify useful structural relationships across physical scales without presuming that every system—or any system—is composed of Planck-scale Quantized Dimensional Cells (QDCs).

QDL distinguishes mathematics proven under defined axioms, software that implements those tests, hypotheses that connect models to nature, and physical claims established by independent experiments. The public V2.2 synthesis does not constitute experimental proof of a Unified Field Theory.

Cross-Scale QDL: A Testable Program Without a QDC Assumption

The central question is whether QDL is useful as a structural method outside any proposed microscopic substrate. An affirmative result would establish an application of a method; it would not automatically establish a universal physical ontology.

The Distinction Between Representation, Closure and Dynamics

Representation: identify real system variables, physical dimensions, quantity kinds, conservation relations, reference frames and measurement procedures. A five-coordinate ledger assignment must be specified and justified; it cannot be chosen after the result merely to force closure.

Closure: evaluate whether mapped quantities satisfy a declared QDL condition, such as C(w)=0. Establish whether that conclusion follows trivially from ordinary dimensional analysis, conservation, algebraic identities or the chosen mapping.

Dynamics: only when independent physical equations or empirical data warrant it, assess whether QDL provides an additional constraint, quantitative prediction, classification or failure detection not already supplied by established methods.

Scope rule: dimensional resemblance and recurrence alone are insufficient. Closed-loop systems are candidates for tests, not presumed instances of QDL physics. No Planck-scale QDC is required for a purely mathematical or metrological cross-scale test.

Micro and Quantum Scale

Laboratory and Engineering Scale

Macro and Astronomical Scale

Exact QDL Mathematical Foundation

These statements hold under specified mathematical definitions. They do not establish that nature is compelled to obey QDL closure.

Five-Axis Primitive and Rank-Four Closure

The integer-ledger vector is:

w = (L₁,L₂,L₃; F₁,F₂) ∈ ℤ⁵

Define the strict closure condition:

C(w) = 2(L₁+L₂+L₃) − 3(F₁+F₂) = 0

Every solution has a unique integer parametrization:

w = (k+p, k+q, k−p−q; k+t, k−t)
k,p,q,t ∈ ℤ

The projected sums are (3k,2k), corresponding to powers of the dimensional monomial L³F².

The entries are ledger coordinates, not five measured spacetime dimensions. The full closure lattice is distinct from the calculator's stricter default subgroup w=k(1,1,1,1,1).

Native Roots and Reversible Contacts

For relative variables (p,q,t), select:

Q(p,q,t) = p² + pq + q² + t²

The integer unit shell Q=1 contains eight roots: six in the A₂-type relative L sector and two in the A₁-type relative F sector.

For an allowed relative transfer δ, CONTACT-37 defines:

Tδ(x,y) = (y+δ, x−δ)
Tδ² = I

The pair sum x+y is conserved. An explicit three-contact word performs complete exchange within the declared algebra.

Formal reversibility and conservation are not evidence that QDC contacts occur in nature.

Finite Geometry and Isotropic Frame

A specified 16-node model graph has 27 edges and 12 independent cycles, with calculable topology and adjacency spectra.

A prescribed 30-loop frame satisfies:

ΣC C Cᵀ = 56 I₃

An exact identity within a defined network is not proof of a Planck-scale physical structure.

Representation-Theoretic Correspondences

The 3+2 partition and traceless coefficients connect with A₂ ⊕ A₁ root geometry within the SU(5) algebra. Conditional work addresses one-generation 15-Weyl content, hypercharge normalization and anomaly checks.

Correspondences alone do not uniquely select Standard Model gauge dynamics, masses, flavor structure, coupling constants or the physical state.

Phase IV — Emergent Universality

Phase IV tests whether independently specified microscopic contact choices could produce the same nontrivial observable long-wavelength theory. Universality has not been demonstrated for physical QDCs.

In tested Gaussian/quadratic contact families, the bulk wave equation was conditionally shared, but an L/F junction parameter remained marginal and affected observable scattering:

Conditional bulk: ẅ + c² ℒGw = 0

A specified junction family exhibited parameter dependence of the form:

P(r) = 2r / (3+r)²

Nontrivial junction probabilities did not converge to a common value in the investigated long-wavelength regime; tested positive junction stiffness yielded trivial total reflection in the relevant limit.

Disposition: MPD, CONTACT-37, CHC, QDL-EU-01 and EU-01F remain mathematical checkpoints. Further speculative Gaussian variants are on HOLD absent independently justified new physical dynamics.

This restricted model-family result does not rule out all microscopic universality classes, and it does not constrain independent cross-scale structural-admissibility tests.

Evidence Definitions and Claim Boundaries

Exact: proven under declared axioms. Conditional: depends on added operators, metrics, dynamics or physical assumptions. Restricted obstruction: a limitation within a specified model class. Cross-scale candidate: an application proposed for independent testing. External benchmark: established non-QDC physics. Physical open: missing carrier, interaction or observational connection. Independent empirical: externally measured, controlled, QDL-distinguishing physical evidence.

The Institute does not claim that QDCs have been observed, that all cyclic or closed-loop phenomena instantiate the QDL lattice, that a unique parent law derives the Standard Model and general relativity, or that two independent QDL-specific no-fit physical predictions have been confirmed.
