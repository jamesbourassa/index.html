Fundamental selector still open

Open Physical-Closure Problems

The main problems separating the current candidate from a completed physical UFT.

1. Unique physical vacuum Select the physical Lorentz/vacuum orbit without target fitting or an arbitrary added coefficient.
2. Normalized parent action / amplitude layer Fix the dimensionless cross-sector coefficients and matter–gravity normalization from a deeper selection principle.
3. Absolute physical scale Derive the common causal/time normalization and absolute scale rather than merely calibrating them.
4. Full Yukawa and flavor dynamics Derive Yukawa matrices, flavor amplitudes, mixing structure, and observed generation phenomenology from the same completion law.
5. Fermionic exchange Derive identical-particle exchange statistics for the physical QDC excitation. Half-integer rotation alone is not sufficient.
6. Particle ontology and binding Demonstrate that stable one-QDC and three-QDC states reproduce the complete observed lepton/hadron quantum and dynamical properties.
7. Global chiral measure Complete the global determinant section/measure across all admitted gauge components. The QM5R3 sequence is the active route.
8. Large-volume transfer Complete the continuum-certified L=4 atlas and then establish the separately preregistered native-volume L=6 transfer.
9. Distinctive prediction Produce a parameter-free dimensionless observable that was not used in constructing or calibrating the theory and survives comparison with nature.
10. Unaffiliated replication Obtain a sealed, independent external return on a frozen QDL/QDC prediction or exact model fingerprint.
