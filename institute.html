Program Orientation

The Institute frames QDL not as a replacement for established theories, but as an upstream structural screen on admissible representations. In that sense, the research program is methodological as well as physical: it asks whether dimensional and closure structure impose stronger constraints than standard homogeneity alone.

The current research sequence is deliberately organized by maturity level: strict theorems, conditional reconstructions, restricted minimality claims, constrained branches, open residuals, and falsification tests are separated rather than collapsed into a single overclaim.
