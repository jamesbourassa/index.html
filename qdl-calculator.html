QDL Admissibility Calculator

The QDL Calculator is an executable demonstration of the same closure logic used in the research program: candidate structures are tested not only for dimensional balance, but for admissibility under declared closure rules.

In the QDL Lattice interpretation, space is modeled not as absolute emptiness, but as a closure-compatible lattice of recurrence. Particles are persistent localized modes of that same structure, and geometry is investigated as a possible large-scale response of collective closure stress. This tool shows the structural-admissibility layer of that program in a public, editable form.

How this fits the QDL Lattice picture

The calculator does not try to simulate microscopic lattice dynamics. It demonstrates the upstream admissibility check: before a model, operator, or measurement relation is trusted, QDL asks whether its declared structure closes under the chosen ledger rule.

QDL Lattice → Declared vector → Closure rule → Admissibility

How to read the output

Ledger vector: the integer 5-slot representation of the expression in the canonical basis.
Exact membership: passes only when v = kq for some integer k.
Transverse remainder: the part of v not captured by q; nonzero remainder means non-closure.
Δ: a modular diagnostic that can be used as a selection-rule style summary.
